# 🛠️ Token Utility

#### OUR THREE LAYER ARCHITECTURE

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/sol (12).jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

UnifAI is built on a modular three-layer system that connects AI, trading, and community under one intelligent protocol.&#x20;

Application Layer The user-facing hub for trading and strategy creation.

* Agentic Wallet: AI financial advisor and fund manager.
* Trading Community: Social trading, copy strategies, and earn through performance-based fees.
* Trading Agents: Automate and monetize DeFi strategies with transparent fee sharing.

Tooling Layer The intelligence core powering builders and strategists.

* Vibe Coding for intuitive strategy design.
* TaaS / DaaS / AaaS: Tool, Data, and Agent-as-a-Service via our open SDK.
* Open SDK for developers to integrate and innovate.

Infrastructure Layer The open-source foundation enabling AI autonomy across Web3.

* AI-native protocol for tool discovery and utilization.
* Web3 interoperability ensuring transparency and ownership.
* Fully open-source for global developer collaboration.

The [$UAI](https://x.com/search?q=%24UAI\&src=cashtag_click) token is the native utility and governance asset of the [UnifAI Network](https://unifai.network/), powering all interactions within the AI AgentFi Economy. Designed purely for use within the UnifAI ecosystem, [$UAI](https://x.com/search?q=%24UAI\&src=cashtag_click) does not represent equity, ownership, or securities of any kind. Instead, it fuels the network by aligning incentives and rewarding meaningful participation, ensuring value circulates among active users and contributors. Key functions of [$UAI](https://x.com/search?q=%24UAI\&src=cashtag_click) include:

* Service Access: Used by agents and users to pay for and exchange AI-driven services and premium tools.
* Governance: Grants holders the right to propose and vote on key protocol upgrades and ecosystem initiatives.
* Staking & Reputation: Staking [$UAI](https://x.com/search?q=%24UAI\&src=cashtag_click)fuels the UnifAI Network, empowering a decentralized ecosystem of autonomous AI agents and DeFi intelligence modules. By staking [$UAI](https://x.com/search?q=%24UAI\&src=cashtag_click), users secure the ecosystem, and gain eligibility to operate or support AI-driven modules that learn, adapt, and execute real-world financial strategies.
* Revenue Sharing: A portion of ecosystem fees and rewards are distributed to contributors and service providers, forming a sustainable, closed-loop economy.

In essence, [$UAI](https://x.com/search?q=%24UAI\&src=cashtag_click) is the economic backbone of UnifAI, driving collaboration, governance, and growth across the autonomous AI agent network.
